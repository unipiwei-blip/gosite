FAQ

Questions before getting started

Straight answers about scope, timing and indexing.

What is included in the ¥999 bilingual site?

One website delivered in Chinese and English, including page structure and copy, language annotations (hreflang), index-ready metadata and a sitemap. Domain, hosting and third-party fees are billed by those providers.

How fast can a site go live?

With content and page structure confirmed, a standard static business website can go live the same day. Larger content sets or extra pages are scheduled during scoping.

What does index-ready mean?

Every page ships with semantic headings, a unique title and description, canonical and hreflang tags, structured data, a robots.txt file and an XML sitemap. Copy is written so search engines and AI answer engines can quote the facts directly.

Do you optimize for keywords in different countries?

Yes. Headings, descriptions and body copy are written around the search terms and phrasing used in each target market, with matching language and regional annotations.

Can more languages be added later?

Yes. A site can start with Chinese and English and expand to additional languages as new markets are added.

How are updates handled?

Send the text, images or new pages. Edits are published the same day in most cases; the China annual plan includes ongoing content updates.
