FAQ / PARTNER FAQ

Deposits and refunds

A full refund applies after 30 clients in one year or on exit.

What is the partner deposit?

Service providers in China deposit CNY 3000; partners outside China deposit USD 500. The deposit is used for settlement at preferential partner rates.

Is the deposit refundable?

A full refund is available when a partner successfully serves 30 or more clients within one year, or when the partner chooses to exit. Terms are confirmed during consultation.

Who maintains local clients?

Local clients are developed and maintained by the service provider in their region. Partner details are published on the website so clients can verify and contact them locally.

What support does a partner receive?

Each partner receives an OEM promotion website, regular promotional poster designs and the same delivery support as GOSITE clients.

How does city exclusivity work?

Several partners may operate in one city at first. Once a partner has developed more than 100 business websites in that city, the city is managed exclusively by that partner.

Which products are covered?

Websites and mini-programs are available now. Business cards, digital humans and a media matrix system are planned and will follow the same partner system, with pricing adjusted as each application launches.
