FAQ / PARTNER FAQ

Deposits and refunds

Straight answers about how the programme works.

What is the partner deposit?

Partners in China deposit CNY 1800; overseas partners deposit USD 300. The deposit is used for settlement at preferential partner rates.

Is the deposit refundable?

A full refund is available when a partner successfully serves 30 or more clients within one year, or when the partner chooses to exit. Terms are confirmed during consultation.

Who maintains local clients?

Local clients are developed and maintained by the partner in their region. Partner details are published on the website so clients can verify and contact them locally.

What support does a partner receive?

Each partner receives a business promotion website of the same type as this site for local marketing. Delivery scope and policy details are confirmed during consultation.
