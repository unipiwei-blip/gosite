CASE ANALYSIS / ANALYSIS

From positioning to indexing

A digital economy think tank serving government, universities and enterprises.

Overview

A think-tank site for three audiences, positioning itself with a single headline and four scale figures before unfolding five business areas.

Structure

Five areas lead into a service matrix, ecosystem, updates and cooperation, so each audience reaches a relevant entry without the sections competing.

Visual & colour

Minimal institutional styling with numbered sections, thin rules and restrained cards. Deep blue and cyan keep long text comfortable; magenta and orange are reserved for emphasis.

Indexing & takeaway

Five keyword groups are distributed across distinct URLs, titles and descriptions; dated updates keep the site active without frequent redesign.
