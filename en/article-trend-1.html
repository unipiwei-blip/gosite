Small Business Websites: From Existing to Being Found

Launching a site is only the beginning. Being hard to find and leaving content stale hurts business more than having no site at all.

From one-off delivery to ongoing service

Website projects used to end when files were uploaded. Companies now ask who will update copy, add sections and publish news, and whether the mobile experience stays good. Annual build-and-maintain plans answer that need.

Readability becomes a baseline

Unique titles and descriptions, factual paragraphs, image alt text, sitemaps and structured data determine whether search engines and AI assistants understand the business. Doing this at launch is cheaper than retrofitting.

Content updates drive long-term value

Fresh cases, news and campaign pages serve both customers and search engines. Separating fixed structure from an update layer keeps the framework stable while content keeps moving.
