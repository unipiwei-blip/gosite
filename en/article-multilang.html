Language Tags and Keyword Layout for Multilingual Sites

Most multilingual problems are not translation quality. They come from language versions being treated as duplicates, or from each version targeting the same keywords.

Published · 2026-11-03

One stable address per language

Chinese and English pages use fixed URLs. Canonical tags point to themselves, alternate links point to the matching language version, and x-default points to the default edition.

Rewrite for the market

Headings, descriptions and body copy follow the search terms used in each target market rather than a literal translation. GOSITE Global organises pages this way for overseas audiences.

Keep entity data consistent

Brand, service and pricing facts stay identical across languages; only the wording is localised. Sitemaps and structured data are updated together whenever a new language is added.
