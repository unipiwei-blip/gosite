Designing Product Pages for Buyers on Their Phone

Overseas buyers often first open a product page on a phone. The layout should answer their questions in the order they ask them.

Published · 2026-11-27
Follow GOSITE Global for weekly notes on launching, indexing and maintaining business websites.

Group products the way buyers search

Organise by application or product category rather than internal team structure. A buyer should recognise their need in the first group of cards.

Show the product clearly

Use a clean main image and detail shots that show material and finish. Keep the original proportions and describe each image with alt text.

Answer questions in order

What it is, where it is used, key specifications, packaging, lead time, minimum order and how to enquire. This order follows a buyer's decision, not a company brochure.

Keep the enquire button close

Place a contact action after the specifications, at the end of the category page and in the footer. A buyer who finds the right product should not have to search for a way to ask.

Test on a real phone

Check that text, images and buttons are comfortable to use on a small screen. A page that only works on desktop loses the buyers who matter most.
