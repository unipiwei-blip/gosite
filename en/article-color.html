Colour and Hierarchy in a Conversion-Focused Homepage

Colour does two jobs on a conversion page: establishing trust and signalling the next action. One primary colour plus one accent colour creates a steadier rhythm than a palette of many bright tones.

Published · 2026-11-07

Primary colour carries trust

Blue is common in technology and education sites because it reads as stable and works behind large amounts of text. White space keeps dense course parameters and job labels legible.

Accent colour stays scarce

Orange and red are reserved for prices, buttons and limited offers. The less often an accent appears, the more precisely it points to the next step.

Hierarchy before decoration

Headline, course cards, labelled roles, classroom photography and student stories follow results, path, evidence, reassurance. Decoration is deliberately kept low so evidence remains the focus.
