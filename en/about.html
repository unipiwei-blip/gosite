ABOUT / OUR STORY

The gap was long-term care, not another website

Fast launch, readable structure and a team that keeps the content current.

A useful business website has to do several things at once: present clear sections, work well on a phone, be found on Baidu and Google, be understood by AI answer engines, and keep its content current. Traditional custom development is slow and expensive to maintain, so many small organisations never get a proper online presence.

AI-assisted planning, copy and structured content, combined with static hosting, make that work considerably lighter. GOSITE packages fast launch, index-ready structure and year-round content care into one annual service for Chinese-language websites; 跨境极站 GoSite delivers bilingual and multilingual sites for overseas markets.

Why the price stays low

The sites are designed and produced by a team of master's and doctoral students in South Korea. The project is led by PI WEI, a PhD candidate in artificial intelligence with years of experience in the internet industry, entrepreneurship and operating multiple online platforms. Developed during doctoral study together with classmates, the project turns section planning, page copy, structured content and launch workflows into a reusable method, which removes repeated design and coordination effort from every engagement.

Static architecture also lowers long-term cost. There is no database or heavy backend; hosting, version history and rollback rely on mature code hosting and static publishing, so day-to-day work stays focused on content rather than server maintenance.

How the work is done

Every page gets its own title and description, and a sitemap plus structured data ship with the site. Where commerce is needed, a WeChat mall mini-program can be added and maintained alongside the website. For overseas markets, keywords follow the language habits of each target country, with language and regional annotations that help Google and AI assistants read every version correctly.
