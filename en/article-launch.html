Why this product exists

Many small organisations do not lack a web page; they lack an online presence that customers can find, that reads well on a phone and that keeps its content current. Custom development is slow and costly to maintain, so sites are often abandoned after launch. GOSITE combines AI-assisted section planning, page copy and structured content with static hosting and year-round care in one annual service.

Positioning and strengths

Chinese-language websites start at ¥399 per year (limited to the first 10 customers each month with complete materials). Standard sites can go live the same day and edits are published the same day. Unique titles and descriptions, a sitemap, canonical tags and structured data ship at launch, and copy is written as facts that Baidu, Google and AI answer engines can quote. Static pages have no database or admin login; HTTPS, CDN delivery and version rollback reduce common risks.

The global edition

The GOSITE Global edition is priced per site: ¥1998 for one site with Chinese and English pages, including everything in the ¥998 plan plus hreflang annotations, country-specific keyword work and global CDN delivery. Additional languages are ¥360 each as full-site translation. Headings, descriptions and body copy follow the search habits of each target market.

Getting better over time

This site is itself an example of the method: seven case studies with desktop and mobile screenshots and a six-dimension breakdown, a content hub ordered by date, and pricing with explicit boundaries. Original analysis on layout, colour, SEO and GEO will keep appearing, and business cards, digital humans and a media matrix system are planned for the service roadmap.
