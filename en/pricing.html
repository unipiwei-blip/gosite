FAQ

Pricing questions

Billing, scope, domains and later extensions.

Is ¥1998 charged per site or per year?

¥1998 is a one-off price for one bilingual site with Chinese and English pages. Additional languages are ¥360 per language, and third-party services are billed by those providers.

What is the difference between ¥399 and ¥998?

Both are the China annual plan for a Chinese-language website with year-round content care. ¥399 is a limited monthly offer for the first 10 customers with complete materials; ¥998 has no quota, allows materials in stages and includes at least one promotional poster design and distribution per quarter.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain registration is not included. Registration through GDIIS at www.gdiis.com is recommended so DNS, publishing and search-console monitoring stay manageable. HTTPS and CDN setup assistance are included.

Can a mini-program be added later?

Yes. A WeChat mall mini-program can be added when transaction, booking or membership features are needed; see the dedicated WeChat Mall page for the full capability list.
