FAQ

Pricing questions

Billing, scope and later extensions.

Is ¥999 charged per site or per year?

¥999 is a one-off price for one bilingual site with Chinese and English pages. Additional languages and country-specific keyword work are quoted separately.

What is the difference between ¥399 and ¥998?

Both are the China annual plan with the same website and year-round content care. ¥399 is a limited monthly offer for the first 10 customers with complete materials; ¥998 has no quota and allows materials to be supplied in stages.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain registration, ICP filing and third-party services are billed by those providers. Setup assistance for DNS and HTTPS is included.

Can a mini-program be added later?

Yes. A WeChat mall mini-program can be added when transaction, booking or membership features are needed, with content maintained alongside the website.
