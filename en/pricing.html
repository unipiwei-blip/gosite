FAQ

Pricing questions

Billing, scope, domains and later extensions.

Is ¥5980 charged per site or per year?

¥5980 is priced per site for one bilingual site with Chinese and English pages, which can be switched or deployed separately. Additional languages are ¥1200 per site, and third-party services are billed by those providers.

What is the difference between Standard and Plus?

Standard at ¥998 a year focuses on display, with a Chinese website, SEO and fully managed care. Plus at ¥2980 adds a Chinese digital business card, social share cards, GEO for AI answers, Baidu indexing within 10 days with filing, and 2 media posts a month.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain registration is not included. Registration through GDIIS at www.gdiis.com is recommended so DNS, publishing and search-console monitoring stay manageable. HTTPS and CDN setup assistance are included.

Can a mini-program be added later?

Yes. A WeChat mall mini-program can be added when transaction, booking or membership features are needed; see the dedicated WeChat Mall page for the full capability list.
